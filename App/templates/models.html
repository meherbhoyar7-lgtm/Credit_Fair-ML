🌙 Non-Linear Manifold Benchmark: K-Means vs. DBSCAN Synthetic Moons Topology
When underlying financial data resides on non-convex or interlocking manifolds, centroid-based algorithms (K-Means, ARI = {{ dbscan_results.moon_ari_kmeans }}) split continuous clusters with linear Voronoi cuts. DBSCAN (ARI = {{ dbscan_results.moon_ari_dbscan }}) leverages density connectedness to reconstruct 98.7% of complex non-linear geometry without geometric deformation.
Analytical Proof Demonstrates that complex financial risk behavior cannot be assumed spherical. Density-based algorithms provide essential topological fidelity for non-linear credit exposures.
